# Sidekick3

Ground-up rewrite of [Sidekick](https://github.com/seekey13/Sidekick), the support-automation addon for Ashita v4 on [CatsEyeXI](https://www.catseyexi.com/).

**Status: in development.** It heals by urgency: the most HP for your MP or TP when someone is hurt, the fastest heal when they're in danger, a boost (Divine Seal, Accession...) when they're critical. It weighs area cures and Regen against single cures, learns what your cures really heal, and has a focus target. It also removes debuffs (with items too, when you turn them on), wakes sleepers, raises, keeps up buffs you turn on, recovers MP and TP (Convert, Devotion, Aspir, Meditate...), looks after your pet (Repair, Spirit Link, sending it in, equipping its oil or food) and heals the party with a rabbit's Wild Carrot on its Ready charges and keeps a Puppetmaster's maneuvers up, keeps a Corsair's rolls up and Double-Ups them, keeps a Geomancer's luopan where it's needed, keeps a Rune Fencer's runes up, keeps a Ninja's shadows up with the tools you carry, casts only the blue magic a Blue Mage has set and spreads its buffs with Diffusion, heals with a Dancer's waltzes around the stance you keep up, and can follow a member and rest when there's nothing to do, for you, your party and trusts, your alliance, and players outside it you track. Sidekick v2 can stay loaded alongside it, but don't run both at once.

## What's different

v2 described every job's spells and abilities by hand in 22 job files. v3 reads them from the game: the client's spell and ability data gives levels, costs, recasts, ranges and targets, and the server's learned-spell and ability lists say what your character has. All that's left by hand is [core/knowledge.lua](core/knowledge.lua), one row per spell family describing what it is *for* (heal, buff, cure a status...), shared by every job.

## Use

```
/addon load sidekick3
/sk3                    open or close the window
/sk3 help               the command list
/sk3 plan               what it would do right now, without doing it
/sk3 start | stop       automation on / off (always off after a load)
/sk3 start | stop Name  the same for another Sidekick3 session on this PC, by its character's name; "all" for this one and every other
/sk3 buff Haste p1 b0   keep a buff up on those targets (me, p1-p5, b0-c5 for the alliance, a tracked player's name); "off" turns it off
/sk3 buff Barfira       for pick-one groups (bar-elements, enspells, storms) name the spell; a Japanese client may name it in Japanese
/sk3 buff               list the buffs that are on
/sk3 focus Name | off   heal this member first, on its own HP lines
/sk3 follow Name | off  follow this member
/sk3 rest [on | off]    rest for MP when there's nothing to do
/sk3 profile [Name]     load a settings profile (default: back to your own settings); with no name, list them
/sk3 track [Name]       look after a player outside your alliance too: your target, or by name; else list them
/sk3 untrack Name | all stop tracking them
/sk3 state              what Sidekick reads from the game: party, trusts, buffs, target, flags
/sk3 catalog [unknown]  what it can automate for your job, by role (or what it can't)
/sk3 log                print each action as it's sent (on while in development)
/sk3 dump               write the client's spell/ability/status data to tests/data/
```

The HUD (`/sk3`) shows the party as Sidekick reads them, and what it will do next:

- **A card per member**: HP and MP bars, the name, and the slot (ME, P1-P5), then one per tracked player (T1, T2..., HP only). The name turns red under your critical line, the slot under your Recover MP line. Hover a card for its HP and MP %, distance and statuses, and whether it's a trust, tracked, KO or away. Your own card's HP bar has the icons that start and stop automation and open SK Settings; so does the card of another Sidekick3 session on this PC, for that session (see Boxing).
- **The alliance's other parties** under the cards: a thin HP bar each, with its slot (B0-B5, C0-C5) and name, each party after a gap; hover one for its HP %.
- **An arrow** at the member the next action is for, labelled like `Heal:Cure IV`: yellow while running, grey while stopped (what it would do). An action on you, your pet or the enemy points at your own card.
- **The title row**: hover it for your job, what Sidekick is doing or why it's waiting (casting, moving, safe zone...), your battle target and pet. Its icon closes the HUD.

Drag the HUD where you want it; Lock position keeps it there.

**SK Settings**, from the cog on your card, has every setting under collapsing headers, each section with its on/off switch:

- **Profiles**: named snapshots of your settings (Dynamis, EXP...), each job pair with its own. Type a name and Save (with a profile loaded, a new name renames it; never over another profile), Save as (over one of that name too), New (every setting back to its default) or Delete; click one to load it. Your settings stay the working copy: changes after a load are kept, but reach the profile only when you Save it, and Default brings back the settings you had before you loaded one. A profile never changes the HUD, the Japanese client, following, the focus member, who healing skips or who Entrust goes on.
- **Follow**: who to follow and how far they may get.
- **Heal**: healing, with its HP lines (danger, critical, and the focus member's own), gear potency and who to heal: the alliance's other parties too when you tick Heal the alliance, each of their parties then on a line of its own to untick; raising.
- **Remove**: waking sleepers (when one of your heals can), and debuff removal: spells, then items (Echo Drops, Remedy...) with how many you carry.
- **Buff**: Hold area buffs for the group, so Protectra, a bar-element or a song waits until the players in your party who lack it are near you (trusts follow and never hold it), and, while one waits that someone near you lacks too, says "Gather together for <spell>" in party chat every 5 seconds (one far player missing it is waited for quietly; untick Ask them to gather to always hold quietly); then each row picks a tier ("Auto" casts the best you can) or, for pick-one groups like bar-elements and enspells, the spell; and the targets, ME and P1-P5, and when the spell can go outside the party, T1... for tracked players and a line each for the alliance's other parties, B0-B5 and C0-C5. On a Scholar, a row's stratagem chips fire one just before its spell: Pen (Penury) and Cel (Celerity) for white magic, Per (Perpetuance) for white enhancing, Par (Parsimony) and Ala (Alacrity) for black, each only under its Arts and while a charge is left; Acc (Accession) instead casts the spell on you for everyone in your party near you whenever one of the row's targets there lacks it (as v2), and waits for the group like an area buff. **Hold**, after a row's chips, makes its spell wait while one of them can't go with it (on recast, too few charges for all of them, the wrong Arts), as v2's Hold for Stratagem; the row then says what it waits for; without it (the default) the spell goes alone. Under Heal, Keep a stratagem for healing (off by default) stops buff rows spending the last charge, so healing has one for Accession or Rapture. A spell that needs an Addendum you don't have up (Raise II, Reraise, Cursna...) goes through Enlightenment under Dark Arts, fired for it by itself. On a Corsair, **Rolls** keeps the rolls you pick up on you (Roll 2 on a Corsair main job only; none is picked at first) and Double-Ups the last one rolled by the Risk you pick, as v2: Lowest banks the lucky number and never risks a Bust past 5; Medium (the default) also chases lucky while it's one die away and doubles off the unlucky number up to 8; Highest goes for 11 while Fold is ready to undo a Bust. Every risk turns 10 into 11 with Snake Eye, and Fold clears a Bust whenever it's ready. Nothing rolls while you're Invisible. A new roll waits until the last one is done doubling, and Hold area buffs for the group holds a roll too, for the players in your party within its area (about 8 yalms). Each die, total and Bust is printed while the action log is on, and resting waits while a roll is still being doubled. On a Bard (main or sub), **Songs** keeps the songs you pick up, by name, two per target on a Bard main job and one on a subjob (a Bard's songs stand that many on each player; one more pushes out its oldest), as v2's Area and ME/P1-P5 buttons: **Area** songs are sung as they are, on you and everyone in your party near you (about 10 yalms), whenever someone there lacks one; **ME** and **P1**-**P5** are each party slot's own, sung on whoever is in it alone through Pianissimo (Bard 20), fired just before the song. A member with songs of their own keeps only the slots those leave for the area ones, so with two of their own no area song is sung for them. Two songs of one kind (Victory and Advancing March, Valor Minuet IV and III) stand side by side, each wanted on its own; with one of them up, the one sung less lately goes. Area songs come first: while one is owed and can't go (its recast, held for the group), the songs on one member wait, since it would push them out; while Pianissimo is up, the area songs wait for the song it is for. Nothing is sung while you're Invisible, and Hold area buffs for the group holds an area song for the players in your party who lack it and have no song of their own picked. Nightingale and Troubadour stay buff rows, kept up before the songs. On a Geomancer main job, **Geomancy** keeps a luopan down: the **Geo** row's (pick the spell, and its targets: the luopan goes on the first of them who lacks its aura, and waits while they're on the move, since it stays where it lands) or, in combat, the **Geo debuff** row's on your battle target (waiting while it moves; Full Circle first takes the slot from another luopan, only when the debuff could follow: in range, off recast, and short of MP by no more than Full Circle gives back). **Full Circle** recalls the luopan once whoever it went on is further than Recall past (10 yalms) from it (or has left), or once it no longer fits the Geo row's spell or targets after you change them, so it goes down again on the first of the Geo row's targets who lacks it, and a debuff's once combat has been over for Keep after combat (5 seconds; a new battle target in that time keeps it). **Blaze of Glory** goes just before a luopan, and **Entrust**, with a member and an Indi- spell picked, puts that Indi- on them whenever they lack its aura within 20 yalms. The Geo rows are off until you turn them on; Indi- spells on you stay buff rows. Under Recover, **Radial Arcana** spends the luopan for MP under your MP line, as v2: one that's cheap (75 MP or less), nearly spent or put down for it, once you're within 10 yalms of it; a dearer one is recalled first (Full Circle gives MP back) and a Geo-Voidance put down on you to spend instead. On a Rune Fencer (main or sub), **Runes** keeps the runes you pick up, one per slot your RUN level gives (1 at 5, 2 at 35, 3 at 65; the same rune twice keeps two up), each slot naming the rune by what it does: **Idle** by the element it adds, the rest of the time; then a set each for **Vallation**, **Valiance** and **Pflug** (by the element it resists, or for Pflug the ailments), as v2. Once a ward is ready, its set takes the slots: its runes go up one at a time, and in combat the ward fires (out of combat its set waits up for the pull, so a long recast isn't spent on nothing); with it on recast, Idle takes them back. Untick a ward to leave it to you; a set with nothing picked takes nothing. Valiance waits while Vallation is up (it would do nothing), Vallation while Valiance is (it would replace it with less), both under Liement. Lunge, Swipe, Gambit and Rayke are yours: the runes go back up after. Nothing goes while you're Invisible. On a Rune Fencer main job, a row of white enhancing magic (Protect, Phalanx, a bar-element...) has an Emb chip that fires **Embolden** just before its spell; while it's on recast the spell goes without, or waits with the row's Hold on (v2's Hold for Embolden). On a Ninja (main or sub), Utsusemi, Tonko and Monomi each spend their tool from your inventory (Shihei, Shinobi-tabi, Sanjaku-tenugui; not the wardrobes), or on a Ninja main job a Shikanofuda: the row shows how many casts you carry and waits with none. **Cast Utsusemi at one shadow** (off by default, as v2's Cast with 1 Shadow) recasts it while its last shadow is up instead of once it's gone; for Ichi, which can't replace Ni's shadows, that shadow is cancelled a second into the cast so the new ones land. Sange needs shuriken worn: the best you hold that you can wear is equipped first in combat, as a pet's oil. On a Blue Mage (main or sub), only the blue magic you have set shows and is cast: the server sends the set when you zone or change a spell, so after loading Sidekick mid-session there is none until then. Harden Shell, Pyric Bulwark and Carcharian Verve are never set: on a Blue Mage main job Unbridled Learning goes just before each, by itself, and the spell waits while it's on recast. On a Blue Mage main job, a row of blue magic that goes on you alone (Cocoon, Refueling, Memento Mori...; not Diamondhide, already an area spell) has a Dif chip that fires **Diffusion** just before its spell, so it lands on everyone in your party near you whenever one of them lacks it, as Acc does for a Scholar; when only you lack it the spell goes without, keeping Diffusion for when the party needs it. While Diffusion is on recast the spell goes on you alone, or, with the row's Hold on, waits while someone else near you lacks it. On a Dancer, Saber Dance and Fan Dance are buff rows (in combat; either one up counts for both, as v2) and so are the sambas (one picked, in combat only, as Presto is); while Saber Dance is up no waltz goes, and while Fan Dance is up no samba, since the server refuses them: their rows say `blocked by Saber Dance` and healing uses your other heals. Under Trance the waltzes and Aspir Samba need no TP, as on the server, so a waltz then heals at any TP and costs nothing. No Foot Rise tops up your finishing moves and Reverse Flourish (under Recover) spends them at five or more.
- **Pet**, for a pet job: its HP line, and its care and send rows. Sending the pet in is opt-in, row by row. Repair, Maintenance and Reward need their oil or pet food in your ammo slot: when one would go but for that, the best you hold in your bag or a Mog Wardrobe that your main job can wear is equipped first (the highest level up to yours), as v2; oils are worn on a Puppetmaster main job only. On a Puppetmaster (main or sub), **Maneuvers** keeps the maneuvers you pick up on you while your automaton is out, one per slot, by element (the same element twice keeps two up; none is picked at first), as v2: none under Overload, none while you're Invisible, and only on your automaton (a jug pet, avatar, wyvern or luopan out under a PUP subjob gets none). A care row whose ammo you hold says it equips it; one that doesn't show after three tries is given up on, so the next row can go. On a Beastmaster (main or sub), a rabbit's Wild Carrot is one of your heals (under Heal) once two Ready charges are banked (3 at most, one back every 30 seconds): it heals everyone near the pet, only when someone is under your danger line, so chip damage leaves the charges for the pet's attacks.
- **Recover**: MP and TP lines (and the TP Chivalry spends from); resting for MP out of combat after a few quiet seconds, standing at full MP or when the leader leaves.
- **Tracked**: the players you track, each with Untrack, and Track for your target when it's a player outside your party and alliance.
- **UI / Log**: the HUD's bar and font sizes, spacing, panel opacity and lock; the action log and the last 10 actions sent, and why; Japanese client, shared by every job pair as the HUD is: spell, ability and item names (and the gather line) go out in Japanese, and Sidekick warns once in chat about any name the client has no Japanese spelling for, since it then goes out in English.

Each row turns a family on or off and says whether it's ready or why not.

**Tracked players** are looked after like members with whatever reaches outside a party: Cure and Curing Waltz, Protect, Shell, Haste, the -na spells, raises, following. Regen, Refresh, Erase and Healing Waltz can't go on them, and an area spell cast from you or a member (Curaga, Esuna, Protectra) lands on your own party only, so it never counts them. Only their HP % can be read: Sidekick `/check`s them for their level to estimate their max HP (they see that you examined them, as in v2; an /anon player is sized like you). Tracking lasts until Sidekick3 is reloaded; a buff row remembers them by name, so tracking them again brings their buffs back.

**Boxing:** every Sidekick3 session on one PC writes a session file under `config/addons/sidekick3/` (its character's server id, jobs and levels, HP, MP and max HP, whether it's running, and the same for its party's players), rewritten every second (four times a second while one controls another, below) and taken as gone after 5 seconds without a write. Each session tracks the players another session lists, but not those in its own party or alliance, with no `/check`: their levels and real max HP come from the file, and so does their HP while your client doesn't draw them. They are untracked 15 seconds after that session closes or stops listing them (at once if they join your party or alliance); players you track yourself are never untracked for you. A session that's zoning keeps its party listed for up to a minute. Untrack one it tracked and it stays off until you track them again. **Remote control:** a member's card (or a tracked player's) that is another session has the run icon and cog too: run starts and stops that session's automation, and the cog opens its own SK Settings, every setting of its own job as that session shows it (titled with its name), where changes go to that session and are applied there within a second or so. Its HUD sizes, Japanese client and action log are its own and stay out of it; you can control any session from any other. `/sk3 start Name` and `/sk3 stop Name` do what that session's run icon does, by its character's name, and `/sk3 start all` and `/sk3 stop all` start or stop this session and every other one, so one macro runs them all. Each session says so in its own chat once it takes the call. Stop works while you're zoning too.

**The alliance's other two parties** are looked after with the same spells: raised, woken, cleared with the -na spells and buffed on their chips, and healed only when Heal the alliance is on (off by default, as in v2: their parties have healers of their own) or when one is your focus; healing then ranks them by urgency alone, like your party. An area spell lands on its target's party alone, so each party is weighed on its own: Curaga, Esuna and Protectra cast from you or a member never reach them, while a Cure spread by Majesty or Accession and aimed at one of them covers their party near them. Their statuses come from what the game reports around you, as a trust's do. A mob another party of the alliance claimed has no `<bt>` in the client, though you fight it too: Sidekick then takes the nearest one within 21 yalms claimed by anyone in the alliance as your battle target, as v2 did, and aims at it by its id.

The UI is one replaceable file: put your own in `ui/custom.lua` and it's used instead. [ui/README.md](ui/README.md) documents what a UI is given and what it can change, with an example.

Settings are kept per job pair in `config/addons/sidekick3/<character>/WHM_BLM.lua` (its profiles too), and the HUD's in `ui.lua` beside them, shared by every job pair. Healing, removal, waking, raising, recovery and pet care are on by default; buffs, items, sending the pet in, following, resting and healing the alliance only when you turn them on.

## Develop

Needs `luajit` and `luacheck`.

```
luajit tests/run.lua      unit tests, against the dumped client data in tests/data/
luacheck .
```

`tools/parity.lua` checks the catalog against v2's job files. Run it from a v2 checkout next to this one: `cd ../Sidekick && luajit ../Sidekick3/tools/parity.lua`.
